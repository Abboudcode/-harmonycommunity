Ghassan — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Ghassan is a learning app for children ages 1–4, with tap-and-hear lessons and simple games in a real recorded teacher voice. We respect your family's privacy: this app does not collect any personal data.

Data we collect

None. Ghassan has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. Nothing about you, your child, or your use of the app is sent to us or to any server.

Works fully offline

All lessons, games, pictures, and voice recordings are built into the app. Ghassan works completely offline and never connects to the internet to collect or send information.

Children

Ghassan is designed for young children and collects no data from anyone, including children. There are no external links, no chats, and no social features in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
