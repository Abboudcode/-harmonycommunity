Budget Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Budget Tracker is an app for tracking household bills, paychecks, expenses, and credit cards. We respect your privacy: your budget is yours. Amounts are hidden by default in the app (tap a card to see its number), and your data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or tracking.

Data we collect

Without an account: nothing. The app works fully without signing in, and everything you enter is stored only in the app on your device. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

With an account (optional sign-in): if you choose to create an account so your budget can be backed up and restored on another device, we store:

  • Your email address — to sign you in.
  • Your budget data — the bills, income, expenses, and credit cards you enter. This is stored as your own private record, so it can sync between your devices.
  • Your account ID — an internal identifier that ties your budget record to your account.

Account data is stored in our backend (Supabase) and is used only to run the app — signing you in and syncing your own budget. Each account can only see its own data.

What we do not collect

We do not collect location data, and we do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking in the app. Nothing you enter is sold or shared with anyone.

Data stored on your device

When you are signed out, your budget is stored only in the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. When you are signed in, your budget is also backed up to your account so you can restore it on another device. When you sign out, the app first backs up your latest changes, then removes your budget from that device — the copy stored with your account remains there until you ask us to delete it (see below), and signing back in restores it to the device.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and the budget stored with it, email moh_aboud@hotmail.com from the email address you signed up with, and we will delete the account and its stored budget.

Children

Budget Tracker is a household budgeting app for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
