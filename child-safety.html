Child Safety Standards

How Harmony Community protects children and responds to child safety concerns — in plain language.

Zero tolerance

Harmony Community has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Content or behavior of that kind is strictly prohibited on Harmony, in any form. Last updated: November 9, 2026.

A service for adults

Harmony Community is a service for adults sharing family stories, and our target audience is adults (18+). It is not directed at children. Accounts and content that violate child safety are removed, and the accounts responsible are banned.

Reporting a concern

Every story and comment in Harmony has a Report option. Reports are reviewed by the developer, and content that breaks these standards is removed. If you see something that worries you about a child's safety, please report it in the app, or contact us directly using the child safety contact below.

Reporting to authorities

If content appears to be child sexual abuse material (CSAM), we report it to the appropriate regional and national authorities — such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement — as required by law.

Following the law

Harmony Community complies with all relevant child safety laws, and these standards are part of how we run the service every day.

Child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety questions is mabboud31@gmail.com. This contact is able to speak about Harmony Community's CSAM prevention practices and how we respond to child safety reports.
