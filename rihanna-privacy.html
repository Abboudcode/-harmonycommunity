Rihanna Prayer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Rihanna Prayer is a Shia (Twelver) prayer app with prayer times, azan alerts, a Qibla compass, daily duas, and prayer lessons. We respect your privacy: this app does not collect your personal data.

Data we collect

None. Rihanna Prayer has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Nothing about you or your use of the app is sent to us or to any server.

Location

If you tap "Use my location (GPS)", your device location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your location is never transmitted, stored on a server, or shared. You can also choose your city from the built-in list instead, in which case location is never accessed.

Notifications

Azan alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. They do not involve any server.

Settings stored on your device

Your chosen city, calculation method, azan voice, alert switches, and time adjustments are stored only in the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Videos in Learn Salat

The Learn Salat lessons play videos streamed from YouTube. When you play a video, YouTube (Google) may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. All other parts of the app work fully offline.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
