Rihanna Prayer — Support

Rihanna Prayer gives Shia (Twelver) Muslims accurate Jafari prayer times, azan alerts in four Shia voices, a Qibla compass, the dua for every day with audio, Dua Kumayl, Taqeeb al-Salat for every prayer, and step-by-step prayer lessons.

The azan alert did not sound

1. Open the app and check the switch is ON for that prayer.
2. On your iPhone, go to Settings → Notifications → Rihanna Prayer and make sure notifications and Sounds are allowed.
3. Make sure the silent switch on the side of the phone is off.
At prayer time the alert plays the first 30 seconds of your chosen azan (an iPhone limit for notifications). Tap the notification and the full azan plays inside the app.

My prayer times look wrong

Check your city in Settings → Location, and the calculation method in Settings → Calculation method (Jafari is the default). You can also adjust any prayer by minutes in Settings → Adjust prayer time.

The Qibla compass is off

Move away from metal and magnets, and wave the phone in a figure-8 to calibrate. The compass is most accurate in the installed app.
