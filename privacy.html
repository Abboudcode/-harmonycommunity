Privacy Policy

How Harmony Community handles your information — in plain language.

What Harmony is

Harmony Community is a place to share family stories, read other people’s stories, and respond with kindness. This policy explains what information the Harmony website and app collect and why. Last updated: October 29, 2026.

What we collect

Your account: your email address, your display name (nickname), and your password — passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider and we never see them. What you share: the stories, comments, photos and videos you post, your reactions, your friend connections, stories shared with you, and reports you send us. Technical basics: which stories were viewed, so view counts work. We do NOT collect your location, your contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we show no ads.

Anonymous posting

You can post stories and comments anonymously, or turn on “Always show me as Anonymous” in your Profile. Other people then see “Anonymous” instead of your name. Your account still exists privately so the app can work — anonymity hides your name from people, it does not create a second secret account.

How we use it

Only to run Harmony: showing your stories and comments to the community, counting reactions and views, connecting friends, delivering shared stories, reviewing reports, and keeping the community safe. We never sell your information, and we never share it with advertisers.

Who provides the storage

Harmony runs on Supabase (database, sign-in, and photo/video storage) and GitHub Pages (the website). Those providers process the information above only so Harmony can work. Photos and videos you attach are stored in public community storage so other members can see your stories — do not attach anything you need to keep private.

Your choices and deleting your account

You can edit or delete your own stories and comments at any time, and change your name or anonymous setting in your Profile. You can delete your whole account in the app or website: Profile → Delete my account. That permanently deletes your account, your stories, your comments, your reactions and your profile. It cannot be undone.

Reporting and moderation

Every story and comment has a Report option. Reports are reviewed by the Harmony owner, and content that breaks the rules — bullying, disrespect, or anything unsafe — is removed. If your content is reported, we may remove it to keep the community safe.

Children

Harmony is for adults sharing family stories. It is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are for people old enough to have their own email account.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@harmony.community. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
